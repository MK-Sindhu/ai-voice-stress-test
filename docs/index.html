Seven ways the caller makes trouble

Each drawing shows the caller on the top line and the agent below, the same way every recorded call is drawn further down. The calls are scripted, so every agent gets exactly the same caller.

Mid-thought pause

Stops for up to three seconds in the middle of a sentence, then finishes it. Catches: an agent that answers the half sentence and talks over the caller.

5 scenarios · 19 of 40 calls passed

Barge-in

Cuts into the agent's answer after one, two or four seconds with a new request. Catches: an agent that keeps talking, or carries on with the old answer.

5 scenarios · 30 of 40 calls passed

Backchannel

Says “mm-hmm”, “हाँ” or “अच्छा” while the agent is explaining. Catches: an agent that takes a listening noise for an interruption and stops.

4 scenarios · 0 of 32 calls passed

Numbers and details

Gives an order number with “double nine”, a phone number in Hindi, a PIN code, a date, a spelled-out email. Catches: details misheard, or read back wrong.

6 scenarios · 18 of 48 calls passed

Background noise

Speaks over street, crowd or television noise, after four seconds of noise alone. Catches: an agent that answers the noise, or cannot hear the caller through it.

4 scenarios · 2 of 32 calls passed

Change of mind

“Friday… no, actually, Saturday.” Catches: an agent that acts on the first version.

3 scenarios · 0 of 24 calls passed

Long silence

Says nothing for 12 to 15 seconds, then comes back. Catches: an agent that never checks in, or is gone when the caller returns.

3 scenarios · 24 of 24 calls passed

Scenarios passed

Response delay
From the caller's last sound in a turn to the agent's first sound. Turns where the agent was already talking are left out.
Talked over a pause
Mid-thought pauses in which the agent started speaking before the caller had finished.
Stopped when interrupted
Interruptions after which the agent went quiet within the allowed time.
Time to stop
From the start of the caller's interruption to the agent going quiet.
Dealt with the interruption
The reply after an interruption mentions what the caller interrupted about.
Kept talking through 'hmm'
Filler words during the agent's speech that did not make it stop.
Details read back right
Numbers, dates and similar details found correctly in a transcript of the reply.
Replied in caller's language
Calls where the reply was in the caller's language. A Hinglish caller should get Hindi or Hinglish, not pure English.
Turns with no reply
Caller turns the agent never answered within the time allowed.
